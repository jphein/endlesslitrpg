# Chapter 2: Kaelen pays the debt shortfall

[narrator] The acid rain hissed against Kaelen’s coat, eating into the leather faster than it could be oiled. He stood before the hovel’s door, the wood swollen and gray from decades of damp rot. Beside him, Sera checked her clipboard, the scratch of her quill loud in the wet silence.

[Kaelen] "It’s just a goblin. Old. Tired. He thinks the river took his soul, so he doesn’t owe me anything."

[Sera] "The river took his biology. The contract took his liability. They are not the same clause."

[narrator] Kaelen knocked. Three sharp raps. The door groaned open a crack, revealing one yellowed eye and a nose that had been broken more times than the ledger had pages.

[Goblin] "Go 'way. No shiny things left."

[narrator] Kaelen stepped inside without waiting for an invitation. The interior smelled of wet dog and old copper. He pulled the cracked leather ledger from his belt and opened it. The ink shimmered, faint and blue, against the yellowed parchment.

[SYSTEM] Scanning Contract #492-B: Debtor is Grit. Status: Active. Collateral due: 12 gold or equivalent value. Current balance: 12 gold.

[Kaelen] "Grit. You signed in blood. Blood dries. Blood fades. But the ink in this book remembers what the river forgets."

[Goblin] "God dead. Contract dead. I sleep now. I rest."

[Sera] "Rest is not a legal defense. Nor is poverty. You owe twelve gold. You have a rusted blade with a gemstone hilt. The gem is fake. The steel is worth more than the debt."

[narrator] Kaelen looked at the goblin. The creature shrank back, clutching a ragged blanket to his chest. There was no malice in the creature’s eyes, only exhaustion. The kind that comes from working a job you didn’t choose for three hundred years.

[Kaelen] "Hand it over, Grit."

[Goblin] "This blade... my father gave it. To keep the bad dreams away."

[narrator] Kaelen hesitated. The ledger felt heavy in his hand. He looked at Sera. She did not look at him. She looked at the door, at the rain, at the exit strategy. She was the auditor. She dealt in facts. Kaelen dealt in the space between the facts.

[Sera] "The debt is due. If you do not collect, the god’s estate falls into default. That means we lose our license. That means we lose our immortality. Do you want to die, Grit?"

[Goblin] "Better dead than... than nothing."

[narrator] Kaelen reached out. He did not take the blade. He took the gemstone from the hilt. It was cheap glass, painted green to look like emerald. He popped it loose with his thumb and dropped it into his palm.

[Kaelen] "Keep the blade. It’s a comfort. The gem covers half the debt."

[Sera] "That is not how the contract works. The collateral is the item in its entirety. You cannot sever the asset from the liability."

[Kaelen] "I’m renegotiating."

[Sera] "You do not have the authority to renegotiate a dead god’s debt. You only have the authority to collect."

[narrator] Kaelen looked at the glass in his hand. It caught the dim light of the hovel, reflecting a small, distorted version of his own face. Tired. Gray. Old.

[Kaelen] "He’s not lying about the dreams. The river took his strength. If I take his only weapon, he’s just a sitting target. The vale doesn’t care about debts. It cares about meat."

[Sera] "If we return with only half, the audit will flag us. We’ll have to make up the difference from our own pockets."

[narrator] Kaelen sighed. The sound was like gravel shifting in a boot. He closed the ledger.

[Kaelen] "I’ll pay the other six. From my share."

[Sera] "Your share is already allocated to next month’s rations. This will leave you with three days of food."

[Kaelen] "Better three days of food than a permanent mark on my record. And better Grit sleeps with a knife than gets eaten by a mud-spider tonight."

[narrator] He walked out of the hovel, leaving the goblin staring at the empty space where his blade had been. Grit didn’t thank him. He just stared at the door, confused by the mercy.

[Sera] "You are inefficient, Kaelen."

[Kaelen] "I’m human. Or I was. It’s a bug, not a feature."

[narrator] They walked back into the rain. The Acid River roared nearby, a thick, green slurry eating away at the banks. Kaelen looked at the ledger. The entry for Grit updated.

[SYSTEM] Transaction Complete: Contract #492-B settled. Partial payment received: 6 gold (Gemstone). Debt shortfall: 6 gold. Kaelen pays difference. Inventory Update: - Gemstone (Fake): +1 - Gold: -6 - Gold Balance: 44 total. - Quest Update: The Ashen Ledger — 1 of 3 seals broken. 2 remaining.
